Reading the avatar

  • Hue per person. The phone number is hashed onto the palette's accent slots, so a contact keeps one colour forever. The usable slots differ per variant: light's base0A is nearly white and is excluded.
  • Resting — glass disc, initials in the hue, ring at 34%.
  • Unread — the ring sweeps once (the bar's travelling edge) and stays at full strength; the list row gains the 2px accent rail.
  • Open — the disc fills with the hue and the initials take the on-accent ink, as ToggleTile already does.
  • Group — two discs stacked, the front one cut out of the ground.
  • No contact card — shortcodes and unknown numbers drop the initials and wear the number itself in base04.
